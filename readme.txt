About 42

42 is a global education initiative that proposes a new way of learning technology: no teachers, 
no classrooms, students learning from their fellow students (peer-to-peer learning), with a 
methodology that develops both computing and life skills. The 42 cursus is free for anyone approved 
through its selection process. The so-called "C Piscine," a 26-day C programming bootcamp, 
is the final stage in the selection process for becoming a "cadet" (42’s student).
